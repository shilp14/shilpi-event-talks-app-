Latest World News Headlines - June 16, 2026

West Asia: U.S.-Iran Peace Agreement
- Strait of Hormuz: Following a peace deal between the United States and Iran to end their war, President Donald Trump stated that the Strait of Hormuz will be "completely open" by Friday, June 19, 2026. Major tanker operators remain cautious, stating that transit will likely take weeks to resume until the agreement is viewed as stable and material.
- Next Steps: The peace deal is expected to be signed in Geneva on June 19, 2026. The text of the memorandum of understanding will be released after that date, and Vice President JD Vance confirmed that nuclear inspectors will return to Iran as part of the agreement.
